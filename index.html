BEYOND THE COLOUR

WHY THIS GAME EXISTS

Colour vision deficiency can make some football kits and other colour-coded information harder to distinguish. Players and coaches benefit from using more than colour: clearly readable numbers, contrasting lightness, different patterns, positions and verbal cues.

This is an educational recognition game, not a medical test, colour-vision simulation, or an official football-kit approval tool. Real matches need clear kit contrast and accessible design from the outset.

Read The FA's colour-blindness guidance.
